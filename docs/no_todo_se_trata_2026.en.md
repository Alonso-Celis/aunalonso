## Not everything is about

Not everything is about the recent purple haircut. Not everything is about the gaze that for brief moments locks onto the camera lens knowing that the viewer is left speechless. Not everything is about the perfect dark jacket. Not everything is about that. It is also the unexpected encounter from days ago. It is also my conversation with the artist you are. It is also your voice that has not ceased since then. Not everything is about that, but yes, this too is what I see in the image, your purple hair, your perfect black jacket, your eyes in exact right place, and the vivid remembrance of your voice

Alonso,
Paris 2026
